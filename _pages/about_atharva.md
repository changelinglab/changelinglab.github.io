Atharva Naik is a PhD student advised by Prof. Carolyn Rose at the Language Technologies Institute (LTI), Carnegie Mellon University (CMU). He also collaborates closely with Prof. Daniel Fried and Prof. David Mortensen. His research focuses on easy-to-hard generalization for LLM agents. He studies how to improve agent capabilities through controllable-difficulty synthetic data generation, post-training with verifiable or meta-level supervision, and inference-time optimization. He applies these methods to coding agents for code quality (code review, linting, refactoring), data science agents (e.g., Kaggle-style challenges), and computational linguistics (e.g., forward reconstruction).
